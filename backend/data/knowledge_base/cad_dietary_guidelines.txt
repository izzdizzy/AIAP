Heart Health Basic Dietary Guidelines & High Blood Pressure Healthy Eating Guide

1. Dietary Fats and Oils:
- Choose healthier unsaturated oils (e.g., olive oil, canola oil, sunflower oil, peanut oil) over saturated fats (palm oil, butter, ghee, lard, coconut milk).
- Avoid trans fats found in deep-fried foods, commercially baked pastries, hydrogenated vegetable oils, and vegetable shortening.
- Replace high-fat coconut milk in laksa or curry with low-fat milk, evaporated milk, or tau-hu (soy milk).

2. Sodium and Salt Reduction (Blood Pressure Management):
- High sodium intake directly increases blood pressure and workload on the heart.
- Limit sodium to less than 2,000 mg/day (approx 1 teaspoon of table salt).
- Reduce consumption of processed meats (sausages, bacon, ham, luncheon meat), canned foods, instant noodles, and preserved items.
- Request "less sauce / less gravy" when eating out at hawker centers. Use herbs, spices, garlic, ginger, and lemon juice for flavor instead of monosodium glutamate (MSG) or heavy soy sauce.

3. Dietary Fiber and Whole Grains:
- Aim for 2 servings of fruits and 2 servings of vegetables daily.
- Choose whole grains (brown rice, oats, wholemeal bread, wholewheat noodles) over refined carbohydrates (white rice, white bread) to lower LDL cholesterol and manage blood sugar spikes.

4. Fish and Omega-3 Fatty Acids:
- Consume oily fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines, Spanish mackerel / batang) at least 2 times per week for cardiovascular protective omega-3 fatty acids.

5. Lifestyle Dos and Don'ts:
- Do participate in at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity per week (e.g., brisk walking, cycling, swimming).
- Do refrain from smoking or tobacco use; smoking accelerates plaque formation in coronary arteries.
- Do limit alcohol intake to no more than 1 standard drink per day for women, 2 for men.
