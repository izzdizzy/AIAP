Singapore Healthcare System & Triage Knowledge Base

1. Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS):
- CHAS Blue: Monthly household income per person <= $1,200 (or AV <= $21,000). Highest subsidies for chronic conditions at participating CHAS GP clinics.
- CHAS Orange: Monthly household income per person $1,201 - $2,000 (or AV $21,001 - $25,000). Moderate subsidies for chronic conditions.
- CHAS Green: All Singaporeans not covered under Blue/Orange. Selected subsidies for chronic conditions under Healthier SG.
- Pioneer & Merdeka Generation: Enhanced subsidies for senior Singaporeans born in 1959 or earlier.

2. Healthier SG Initiative:
- National preventive health initiative encouraging residents to enroll with a regular family doctor (GP).
- Free fully-subsidized health screenings, annual health plans, and subsidised chronic medications.

3. Primary Healthcare & Polyclinics:
- Polyclinics (SingHealth, NHG, NUHS networks) offer one-stop subsidized primary care, blood tests, and chronic disease consultations.
- CHAS GP Clinics provide accessible neighborhood medical consultations with Government subsidies matching subsidy tier.

4. Triage Guidelines & Emergency Response:
- Low Urgency / Routine Monitoring: Schedule routine follow-up with regular CHAS GP or Polyclinic. Maintain lifestyle and prescribed medication.
- Moderate Urgency / Increased Surveillance: Schedule GP/Polyclinic appointment within 3-5 days. Monitor blood pressure, blood glucose, and chest discomfort.
- High Urgency / Immediate Intervention: Seek urgent medical care at nearest Polyclinic or Hospital A&E immediately. Call 995 if experiencing acute chest pain, shortness of breath, severe dizziness, or loss of consciousness.
